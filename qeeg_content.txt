Quantitative Electroencephalography (QEEG) is an advanced test that measures your brain's electrical activity. It analyses brainwave patterns to detect how well different parts of your brain are working — helping identify focus, mood, or sleep issues early. Unlike CT or MRI scans that show structure, QEEG shows how your brain functions in real time. Why It Matters QEEG reveals brain function patterns that are invisible to standard imaging tests, giving doctors a clearer picture of cognitive and emotional health. This helps in early detection, precise diagnosis, and tracking treatment progress for a wide range of neurological and psychological conditions.
